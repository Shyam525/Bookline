Built for businesses that value their time

Every feature answers a real problem. Nothing added for the feature list.

📅

Slot engine that actually works

Real-time availability based on staff hours, buffers and existing bookings. Handles time zones, DST and back-to-back conflicts automatically.

🔒

Zero double bookings

A database-level constraint enforces slot uniqueness. 50 customers booking the same slot simultaneously — only one succeeds. The rest get the next available time.

📱

Reminders that reduce no-shows

Automatic reminders at 24 hours and 2 hours. Templates you control. Delivery log so you always know what was sent and when.

👥

Multi-staff, multi-service

Assign services to specific staff, or let customers choose "any available". Build working hours, breaks and time-off per person.

💳

Deposits and packages

Collect deposits to secure bookings. Sell service packages. All payments handled through Stripe — your money in your account.

📊

Dashboard built for decisions

Revenue, utilisation, no-show rate, top services. Exportable data. Designed to be read in 30 seconds, not studied for 30 minutes.
