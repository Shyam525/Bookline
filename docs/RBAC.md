# Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

Bookline enforces role-based policy authorization server-side:
- **Owner**: Full management rights across organization, staff, services, settings, and billing.
- **Admin / Manager**: Operations management for appointments, customers, and schedules.
- **Receptionist**: Front desk operations, quick bookings, check-in, and rescheduling.
- **Staff**: View personal calendar and manage owned appointments.
