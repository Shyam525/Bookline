# Bookline Staff Management Documentation

## Staff Capabilities & Assignments

Bookline provides comprehensive team member profile management and service assignment mapping:

### Features
- **Profile Management**: Full name, title/role, contact email, phone number, bio, avatar, and timezone.
- **Service Assignment Junction**: `StaffService` mapping determining which services each staff member is qualified to perform.
- **Working Hours & Time-Off**: Weekly recurring hours and date-range time-off exceptions.
- **Overlap Validation**: Enforced validation ensuring no overlapping working windows on the same day.
