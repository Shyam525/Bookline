# Bookline Customer Discovery & Experience Interview Guide

> **Note for Founder / Interviewer**:  
> This interview guide is designed to discover end-customer booking psychology, waiting frustrations, and digital service preferences in Gujarat. Interview individuals who regularly visit salons, barbershops, or beauty clinics (at least once a month). Focus on their real past behavior over theoretical opinions.

---

## 1. Customer Target Demographics

- **Cohort 1: Working Professionals & Students (Ages 18–35)**: Frequent salon/barbershop users who value speed, online convenience, and specific stylists.
- **Cohort 2: Women Seeking Regular Beauty & Skin Services (Ages 22–50)**: Salon & studio clients who book facials, threading, waxing, hair color, and bridal grooming.
- **Cohort 3: Men Visiting Barbershops / Grooming Lounges (Ages 20–45)**: Regular haircut, beard styling, and head massage clients.

---

## 2. Interview Structure & Timing (15–20 Minutes Total)

```
Part 1: Service Frequency & Routine (3 mins)  ──► Establish habits and visit regularity
Part 2: Booking Experience Today    (7 mins)  ──► Identify friction in current methods
Part 3: Salon Waiting & Uncertainty (5 mins)  ──► Measure pain of physical waiting
Part 4: Digital Booking Comfort     (5 mins)  ──► Evaluate direct links vs marketplaces
```

---

## 3. Detailed Question Script

### Part 1: Routine & Preferences
1. "How often do you visit a salon, barbershop, or beauty clinic?"
2. "Do you usually go to the same salon every time, or do you switch between different places?"
3. "Do you have a favorite stylist or specialist whom you specifically ask for, or are you happy with whoever is available?"
4. "On what days and times do you usually prefer getting personal grooming services done?"

### Part 2: How You Book Today
1. "The last time you visited a salon, how did you arrange the visit?"
   - Did you walk in and hope there was a free chair?
   - Did you call them beforehand?
   - Did you message the salon owner or stylist on WhatsApp?
   - Did you book through an app or website?
2. "If you booked on WhatsApp or by phone, how long did it take to agree on an available time slot?"
3. "Have you ever tried calling a salon during peak hours (like a Sunday afternoon) and no one answered the phone? What did you do?"

### Part 3: Waiting Times, Delays & Frustrations
1. "What is the longest you’ve had to sit and wait in a salon reception even though you had called ahead?"
2. "How do you feel when you show up for an appointment and your preferred stylist is in the middle of a 45-minute service for someone else?"
3. "Have you ever walked out of a barbershop or salon because the wait time was too long or unclear?"
4. "How do you handle cancellations or rescheduling when your plans change?"

### Part 4: Online Booking Links & Language
1. "If your favorite salon or stylist had a direct booking link in their Instagram bio or WhatsApp status showing their exact free slots for today, would you prefer using that over calling them?"
2. "When booking online, do you prefer paying online in advance, or paying in-person at the salon via UPI QR / Cash after the service is completed?"
3. "Would having the booking page available in Gujarati make you or your family members more comfortable booking online?"
4. "What would make you hesitate to book an appointment through an online link?"
